🎯 Our Mission

The Smart Food Reader was developed to promote awareness about what we consume every day. Our system leverages AI, image recognition, and data science to analyze food labels and barcodes — helping users make healthier, more informed dietary decisions.

Objectives:

  • ⚙️ Identify food products using AI-based image recognition.
  • 📊 Analyze ingredients for nutritional quality and harmful additives.
  • 💡 Suggest healthier substitutes and alternatives.
  • 🌱 Encourage mindful eating and promote health-conscious choices.
